## 1. North Star + Revenue Model

**North star: paid trunk orders, not followers.** Followers are inventory; orders are the product.

### Four revenue paths (in priority order)

| Path | Mechanic | Why it works here |
|---|---|---|
| **Traffic → site orders** | Reels → profile → bio link → checkout. The FREE virtual simulator is the bridge: "open one free right now" converts a scroller into a site visitor with zero commitment. | The sim is a try-before-buy dopamine loop no competitor page has. |
| **DM-to-order** | Every "price?" / "real?" comment gets a DM within 60 min: link + a coupon (`FIRST50` for first-timers). Manual close, high intent. | Indian buyers buy in DMs. A coupon in the DM creates urgency + tracking. |
| **Drops & scarcity events** | 2 limited drops in 60 days: capped units, countdown stories, drop-only coupon, "sold out" recap post. | Mystery-box demand is spiky; scarcity converts fence-sitters and gives content. |
| **UGC / affiliate loop** | Buyers must post unboxing to The Wall + tag the page for bonus gems; later, 10% coupon codes per micro-creator (their code = their commission proxy). | Every order manufactures the next post's social proof. |

### 60-day targets (base case, math shown)

| Funnel step | Assumption | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Feed items published | 1/day × 60 + stories | 60 posts/reels |
| Total video views | avg ~7k/reel blended (1k floor, few 20k+ outliers), ~50 reels + LinkedIn seed | **~350,000** |
| Profile visits | 6% of views | ~21,000 |
| **Followers** | 15% of profile visits follow | **3,000** (stretch 8,000 with 1 viral reel) |
| **Link clicks** | 10% of profile visits + story link taps + DM links | **~2,500** |
| Site orders (organic) | 2.5% of link clicks (impulse product, ₹999 entry) | ~62 |
| Drop orders | 2 drops × ~20 units | ~40 |
| **Total orders** | | **~100** |
| AOV | mix skews Silver/Gold, some Diamond+ | ₹1,700 |
| **Revenue (60d)** | 100 × ₹1,700 | **~₹1.7L base · ₹3L+ stretch** |

Weekly checkpoints: Day 14 ≥ 600 followers / 5 orders. Day 30 ≥ 1,400 / 30 orders. Day 45 ≥ 2,200 / 60 orders. Miss two checkpoints in a row → escalate per Section 8 rules.

## 9. Compliance Guardrails

Mystery boxes sit adjacent to games-of-chance. Indian state gambling laws + IG's policies both punish gambling *framing* more than the product itself. The armor:

**Words banned everywhere (captions, overlays, bio, DMs):** "bet", "betting", "gamble/gambling", "jackpot", "lottery", "lucky draw" (for paid products), "casino", "win money", "cash prize", "double your money", "guaranteed win", "100% profit", any claimed odds of profit.

**Framing that keeps the page safe:**
- Sell it as **surprise commerce**: "every trunk contains real products" — you are buying sealed products, not a chance at nothing. Never imply a trunk could contain more or less than *products*.
- Never state or imply guaranteed resale value or profit ("₹999 trunk, ₹5,000 value guaranteed" — banned). Show *ranges and past pulls* as facts, not promises: "past pulls include…", "loot pool ranges ₹99–₹99,999."
- The sim and Spin-to-Win are **free games with no cash-out** — always describe them as "free to play, win gems/discounts", never as winnings.
- No age-gating claims needed, but keep content 13+ clean; if any collab targets students, no pressure-to-purchase framing.

**Giveaway rules (both events):**
- Entry must be **free** — never require a purchase to enter (purchase-gated prize draws risk classification as a lottery under Indian prize/chit-fund and state gaming laws).
- Every giveaway caption carries: *"No purchase necessary. Free entry. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram/Meta. Winner announced on [date] on this page; we will never DM asking for payment or card details."*
- Announce winners publicly, deliver publicly (Day-14/45 reels double as proof). Warn followers about fake "you won, pay shipping" scam accounts in stories during every giveaway.

**Platform hygiene:** no engagement-bait phrasing IG flags ("follow or you'll miss out forever"), no third-party follower services, disclose paid meme-page shoutouts with "Paid partnership" label where the feature is available, keep barter collabs labeled #collab or via Collab-post. If a post is ever taken down: don't repost it verbatim; rewrite the caption against this section first.
